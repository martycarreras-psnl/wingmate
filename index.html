01Find your direction

Shape My App

Turn a one-sentence idea into a clear outcome, a sensible scope, and a shared picture of success.

What happens in Shape

Work through users, workflows, exceptions, and acceptance criteria one question at a time, with a recommended answer.

Bring a brand guide or a screenshot if you have one. Visual discovery is optional: “recommend something for me” is a perfectly good starting point.

Leave with: a grounded brief and the first entries in your App Workbook.

Download Shape My App ↓
02Know your foundations

Design My Data

Understand the records, sources, ownership, and access your app needs before committing to a design.

What happens in Data

Review what already exists, what can be reused, and which operations are actually needed. Separate reading data from permission to change it.

Leave with: reviewed data decisions and access boundaries, not automatically provisioned resources or connected business data.

Download Design My Data ↓
03You take the controls

Build with /app

Use Cowork’s native app-building capability to turn the reviewed brief into an explicitly scoped preview.

What happens in Build

You invoke /app in the same conversation. It is a native Cowork command, not a sixth Wingmate skill.

Review the proposed actions and native approvals. App generation can create persistent resources; requesting synthetic data does not guarantee a resource-free build.

Return to a pack skill afterward. Native /app results reconcile into the workbook and journey on that next skill turn—not through background synchronization.

Included in Cowork where available.
No additional Wingmate download.

04Try the awkward cases

Pressure-Test My Prototype

Challenge the workflow and visual direction. Find the gaps while changing course is still inexpensive.

What happens in Pressure-test

Review real scenarios, confusing interactions, and failure paths. Use targeted human UI evidence, such as a redacted screenshot or a specific observed result.

Run safe functional checks only when actual non-browser code or authorized service tools are available. Record gaps and agree on scoped repairs.

Leave with: prioritized findings, evidence, and a clear next iteration.

Download Pressure-Test My Prototype ↓
05Trade guesses for evidence

Prove My App Works

Check the acceptance criteria against what actually happened. Keep unknowns visible, not quietly checked off.

What happens in Prove

Execute safe functional tests through available non-browser app-code or authorized service tools. Test mutations need explicit approval for an isolated, synthetic-data scope.

For UI checks, ask for targeted human evidence. If execution is unavailable, record the check as unverified or blocked. Generated test plans and source review are not test passes.

Leave with: actual results, remaining risks, and honest limits.

Download Prove My App Works ↓
06Land responsibly

Release My App Responsibly

Review the exact build, intended audience, support, and recovery before a human decides to publish.

What happens in Release

Review exposed data and representative-recipient access, unresolved findings, and the plan if something goes wrong. Approval applies to a specific scope and build, not every future change.

Leave with: a release decision and recorded conditions. The skill does not publish, share the app, change permissions, or treat silence as approval.

Download Release My App Responsibly ↓

Small start. Clear next step.

Your idea is enough.

No requirements document to write first. No secrets required. Bring a simple problem you would like to solve.

  1. Download & extract

    Get the skill pack and extract the outer Wingmate-Skills.zip. Inside are five individual skill ZIPs plus companion templates. The outer bundle is not the skill upload.

  2. Add the five skills

    In Cowork, go to Customize → Skills and upload each inner ZIP separately. When updating, preserve your current workbook and journey; replace old skill versions rather than leaving duplicates.

  3. Start shaping

    Activate Shape My App through Sources and share a one-sentence idea. Stay in that same chat for the journey, selecting the next skill when needed.

Try a first sentence like this

“I want an app that helps our team spot manufacturing line problems and manage cases to resolve them.”

An honest preflight check

Useful guidance.
Not a guarantee.

Wingmate is an independent community pilot. Locally validated packaging and interaction behavior are not tenant certification or proof that a model will follow every instruction.

Built for the current Cowork pilot

Current artifact packaging and pilot work target Microsoft Copilot Cowork. File, HTML, clipboard, and tool support vary by environment. Copilot Studio is an intended future validation target—not a supported compatibility or verified import claim.

Evidence without browser automation

Functional tests use only actually available, safe non-browser code or authorized service tools. UI checks rely on targeted human evidence.

No browser or computer-use probes, retries, setup changes, or alternate automation runners. No claim of a test pass without execution. If a check cannot run, that limit stays visible.

You own approvals and publication

The skills provide instruction guidance, not policy enforcement. Real permissions, organizational policy, and native approval controls still apply. Approvals must be explicit and scoped; the skills do not publish apps.

Local files, no added telemetry

This page and the local journey template have no tracking, external assets, or live remote API connections. Wingmate requires no secrets. A website host may still keep ordinary access logs, and work inside Cowork remains subject to Microsoft’s service terms and your organization’s policies.

Use synthetic data for the pilot. Keep credentials and sensitive business information out of shared workbooks, prompts, and screenshots.
