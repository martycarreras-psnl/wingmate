1. Shape the idea Not started

Skill: Shape My App. Clarify who needs the app, what outcome matters, workflow exceptions, scope, and visual direction. Grilling means one useful question at a time, with a recommendation.

Produces: a brief, shared business terms, visual direction, and measurable acceptance criteria. Gate: explicit approval of the current brief.

No brief approval recorded.

2. Review the data Not started

Skill: Design My Data. Reuse authoritative sources, clarify records and relationships, and determine who may see or change what.

Produces: a data/access plan and scoped build handoff. Gate: current brief and data-plan approvals; unresolved live-data capabilities stay blocked.

Source ownership, operations, and exposure are not yet reviewed.

3. Build the approved plan Not started

Native skill: /app. The human invokes the native builder here, after reviewing its proposed actions. This pack does not invoke it automatically.

Produces: an identifiable preview for review, not release proof. Gate: valid brief/data approvals and an explicitly scoped handoff. Do not publish.

Awaiting approved build scope. This card will contain the scoped /app prompt when ready.

4. Pressure-test the prototype Not started

Skill: Pressure-Test My Prototype. Challenge realistic workflows and exceptions, compare the design with your direction, and find functional gaps.

Produces: findings tied to requirements, safe non-browser test results where available, and scoped repairs. Gate: critical findings resolved and prototype approval.

No preview review evidence recorded. Screenshots alone do not prove interactive behavior.

5. Prove it works Not started

Skill: Prove My App Works. Connect each requirement to actual test evidence for the current build. Recheck failed and affected paths after repairs.

Produces: passed, failed, blocked, not-run, and stale results with evidence limits. Gate: required checks have current passing evidence; a checklist is not a pass.

No acceptance execution recorded. Static findings and human reports remain clearly attributed.

6. Release responsibly Not started

Skill: Release My App Responsibly. Review the exact build, audience, data exposure, ownership, support, and recovery before sharing.

Produces: a human-gated release handoff. Gate: current evidence and explicit release approval. Publication is a separate human action and requires evidence afterward.

Release has not been approved; publication is unverified.
